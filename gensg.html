Genetive Singular (gen.sg.)

We decline the word this way when we want to say that smth is of smth else.

In russian it is used with numbers too, because when you say 4 roubles, you are saying 4 of roubles, so you decline рубль like четыре рубля

Masculine and neuter nouns will have an -а or -я appended

If it ends in a consonant then you will add an -а

  • друг (friend) becomes друга (of a friend)

If it ends in ь, й or е, then you will append a -я

  • например рубль becomes рубля

  • музей becomes музея

  • свидание becomes свидания

Neuter nouns that end with -мя will have a genetive -ени, for example время becomes времени

Feminine nouns (or masculine with feminine endings) you will replace for ы or и

If it ends in -а replace for ы

  • москва becomes москвы

If it ends in -я or -ь replace -и

  • неделя becomes недели

  • дверь becomes двери

  • папа becomes папы
